## What Northwind Labs Does

Northwind Labs builds software for **field-service logistics** — the coordination layer that helps small operations teams dispatch workers, track jobs, and manage resources in the field. The core belief is that enterprise-grade logistics tooling should be simple enough that a two-person team can run it from a phone, without specialist IT support.

The project you are joining — **CTMS_Project** — is a **Cinema Ticket Management System**: a web application for managing movies, showtimes, seat bookings, and payments at a cinema. Note that the CTMS codebase is a different domain from the logistics products described in the company handbook; you are working on this system as your assigned project at Northwind Labs.

## Values

These five values appear in the company handbook (`company/handbook.md`) and describe how everyone at Northwind Labs is expected to work:

| Value | What it means day-to-day |
|---|---|
| **Ship small, ship often** | Prefer many small merged PRs over large infrequent ones. Break work down before you start. |
| **Quality is everyone's job** | Testing, review, and correctness are not someone else's responsibility. You own the quality of what you ship. |
| **Write it down** | Decisions, context, and reasoning belong in writing — tickets, PR descriptions, comments, docs. If it was only said in a call, it didn't happen. |
| **Customer first, not customer only** | Users come first in decisions, but not at the expense of maintainability, security, or team health. |
| **Own it end to end** | Follow your work from ticket through deployment and beyond. Don't hand off and forget. |

## Ways of Working

### Code and PRs
- Work is tracked in **GitHub Issues**: `github.com/northwind-labs`
- Pull requests should be **under 400 lines** of diff. If your change is larger, split it.
- Commits must follow **Conventional Commits** format:
  - `feat:` — a new feature or capability
  - `fix:` — a bug fix
  - `chore:` — non-functional changes (build, deps, config, refactor)
  - Example: `feat: add showtime filtering by date`

### Hours and Availability
- **Core hours** are **10:00–15:00 in your local timezone**. Be reachable and responsive during this window.
- Outside core hours, async communication (GitHub, Slack) is the default.

### On-call
- On-call rotation starts at **week 4**, paired with an experienced engineer.
- You will be shadowing before you carry the pager alone.
